What I’m building[STAGE]

The grid is shifting from spinning machines to inverters, and it needs control that can hold itself up and restore itself without waiting for a human in a control room.

Problem
As inverter-based resources displace conventional generation, the grid loses the inertia and voltage support that kept it stable. Restoration after major disturbances is still largely manual, sequential, and slow.
Approach
Grid-forming control architectures that establish voltage and frequency directly, paired with reinforcement learning agents that coordinate distributed resources and restore service autonomously, section by section.
Why now
Inverter penetration, extreme-weather outages, and the load growth from electrification and data centers are converging faster than today’s operator-in-the-loop tools can keep up with.
